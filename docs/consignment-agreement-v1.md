## 2. Ownership, condition, and delivery of cards to Geega Games

**2.1 You keep ownership until a card sells.** Consigned Cards remain your property until they are sold to a buyer. Geega Games holds them as your agent for sale. We do not buy them, and we will not trade them, use them as trade credit, lend them, pledge them as collateral, or treat them as our inventory. When a card sells, title passes directly from you to the buyer.

**2.2 Sale only, never trades.** Consigned Cards are offered for sale for money only. They are never available in the Geega Games trade program, never exchanged for other cards, and never used to settle a trade, even when we also own copies of the same card.

**2.3 Your promises about the cards.** You confirm that:

1. you own each card outright, or are legally authorized to consign it;
2. no other person, lender or estate has a claim to it;
3. to your knowledge, each card is authentic and not counterfeit, proxied, altered, or stolen; and
4. any description you give us is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

If any of these is untrue, we may remove the affected card from sale and return it to you at your expense, and you are responsible for resulting buyer refunds and costs.

**2.4 Shipping cards to us.** You choose how to send cards to us and pay that shipping unless we agree otherwise in writing. Cards are at your risk until our records show we received them. We recommend tracked, insured shipping with cards in sleeves and top-loaders. We will confirm receipt by email.

**2.5 Inspection and acceptance.** After receipt, we inspect each card for identity, printing, finish (foil or nonfoil), authenticity and condition. Our condition grading uses the TCGplayer condition guidelines (Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played, Heavily Played, Damaged). Your descriptions, quantities and grades are not binding on us until we accept them. We may decline any card, including cards below our minimum accepted value of {{min_card_value}} per card, cards we believe are inauthentic, or cards we do not expect to sell. We will list accepted cards on your Consignment Inventory Record and return declined cards to you under Section 7.

**2.6 Condition disagreements.** If you disagree with our grading, tell us within 7 days of the Consignment Inventory Record being posted. We will review the card and send photos. If we still disagree, you may have the card returned under Section 7 instead of listing it.

**2.7 Care and custody.** While Consigned Cards are in our possession, we will store them securely and with reasonable care, kept separate in our records from cards we own. Section 8 describes our responsibility for loss or damage while cards are in our possession.

## 4. Commission and how your proceeds are calculated

**4.1 Commission rates.** Geega Games' Commission is a percentage of the Net Sale Amount of each individual card:

{{commission_table}}

The rate is chosen by that one card's Final Selling Price before any deduction. Cards in the same order are each assessed on their own price, not the order total. These rates apply for the life of this Agreement and can change only under Section 8.5.

**4.2 The calculation.** For each sold card: Final Selling Price, minus Transaction Expenses (Section 4.3), equals the Net Sale Amount. The Net Sale Amount, minus the Commission (the rate from Section 4.1 times the Net Sale Amount), equals the Consignor Proceeds.

**4.3 Transaction Expenses.** Only these actual costs may be deducted, and only to the extent attributable to your card:

1. the marketplace fee the Sales Channel actually charged;
2. payment processing fees actually charged, unless the channel's fee already includes processing (eBay does), in which case no separate processing fee is deducted;
3. outbound postage actually paid to ship the card to the buyer, minus any shipping charge the buyer paid (Section 4.4);
4. the actual premium for additional shipping insurance, only if you approved additional insurance under Section 5; and
5. any other expense you specifically approve in writing for that sale.

We do not deduct packaging supplies, labor, storage, photography, listing time, or our general overhead. No expense is deducted twice.

**4.4 Buyer-paid shipping.** When a buyer pays a shipping charge, that charge is credited against the postage for that package before postage is allocated to any card. If the buyer's shipping charge exceeds the postage, the excess is not added to your proceeds and is not deducted from them.

**4.5 Orders with more than one card.** When one order or package holds several cards (yours, other consignors', or ours), order-level and package-level costs are allocated to each card in proportion to its Final Selling Price compared with the total of all card prices in that order or package. This applies to per-order fees, postage after the buyer-shipping credit, and insurance premiums. Rounding differences of a cent go to the highest-priced card.

**4.6 Rounding and floor.** All amounts are calculated in U.S. dollars and rounded to the nearest cent, with half-cents rounded up. If Transaction Expenses exceed the Final Selling Price, the Net Sale Amount is zero, no Commission is charged, and you owe us nothing for that sale.

**4.7 Example (website sale, estimate only).** A card sells for $200.00 on the Geega Games website, with $6.00 processing, $5.00 postage and $4.50 additional insurance. Net Sale Amount = $200.00 − $15.50 = $184.50. The card sold above $150.00, so the Commission is 10% × $184.50 = $18.45. Consignor Proceeds = **$166.05**. Your actual proceeds use the actual expenses of the actual sale.

**4.8 Estimates are not promises.** Any payout estimate or calculator we provide is for information only. Your proceeds are calculated after each sale from that sale's actual expenses and recorded in your account statement. A later change to estimated rates never changes proceeds already calculated.

## 5. Shipping to buyers, insurance, and carrier loss

**5.1 How we ship.** For domestic sales we normally ship with {{insurance_service}}, which ordinarily includes insurance up to {{included_coverage}} for eligible shipments. We may use another service when it suits the sale better, such as a bubble mailer with tracking or Priority Mail for higher-value packages. Postage is a Transaction Expense under Section 4.3. On TCGplayer and eBay, the channel's shipping rules may control how a card is shipped.

**5.2 Your insurance election.** You choose one of these when you sign. Neither is pre-selected.

- **Option A: Approve additional insurance.** When a package containing your card is worth more than the coverage included with the shipping service, we will buy additional USPS insurance up to the package's value. The actual premium is a Transaction Expense, allocated under Section 4.5. It reduces your Net Sale Amount, and so your proceeds, by roughly the premium less the Commission on it.
- **Option B: Decline additional insurance.** We will rely only on any coverage automatically included with the shipping service. If a package is lost or damaged in transit and its value exceeds that coverage, you bear the uncovered loss of your card as described in Section 5.4. This can mean receiving nothing for a card that was sold and lost.

Your election applies to every sale until you change it in your account. A change applies only to sales made after you change it.

**5.3 How insurance is calculated.** Insurance is bought for the whole package based on the total value of all cards in it, not separately for each card. No additional insurance is bought when included coverage already covers the package. The premium is the rate USPS charges when the label is bought, under its published price schedule then in effect, and is allocated across the cards in that package under Section 4.5. Packages worth more than USPS's maximum insurable amount, or ineligible for insurance, are reviewed by us before shipping and we may ship them by a different service. Insurance does not guarantee that a carrier will approve a claim or pay the full amount.

**5.4 Lost or damaged shipments.** If the carrier loses or damages a package containing your sold card, and the buyer receives a refund:

1. the sale is reversed, and no Commission is charged on it;
2. we file a claim with the carrier for every package where coverage applies, within the carrier's deadlines, and pursue it with reasonable diligence;
3. any claim payment received for your card, up to its Final Selling Price, is treated as the sale price and settled to you under Section 4, as if the card had sold; and
4. any loss above the coverage and claim payment received for your card is borne by you, unless Section 5.5 applies.

If the card is damaged but returned to us, we will give you the choice to relist it at its new grade or have it returned to you.

**5.5 Losses Geega Games is responsible for.** Section 5.4 does not apply, and we will pay you the Consignor Proceeds you would have received on the original sale, when the loss or damage results from our own negligence or misconduct, including improper packaging, a wrong address we entered, failing to buy insurance you approved, or failing to file a claim on time. Nothing in this Agreement limits our responsibility for our own negligence, fraud, or willful misconduct.

**5.6 Records.** For each shipment we record the tracking number, carrier, service, declared package value, insurance purchased and its cost, and any claim and its outcome. These records are visible in your account for shipments containing your cards.

## 6. Buyer inspection, payouts, refunds, and chargebacks

**6.1 Buyer inspection period.** Each sold card has a buyer inspection period of {{inspection_days}} calendar days. We will initiate payment by the next Business Day after proceeds become due. In the example above, payment would be initiated on Friday.

**6.3 How you are paid.** We pay by the method you choose in your account from the methods we offer: {{payout_methods}}. We may combine several cards' proceeds into one payment. A payment is complete only when the payment provider confirms it was sent. You are responsible for giving us accurate payment details. If a payment fails or is returned, we will contact you and resend it once corrected.

**6.4 Holds limited to the affected card.** If a buyer reports a problem with a card within its inspection period, or files a dispute, we hold only the proceeds for that card while we review it. Your other proceeds are paid on schedule. We will tell you about the hold and the reason within 2 Business Days.

**6.5 Refunds and returns.** If a buyer is refunded for a Consigned Card (for example because it was misdescribed, counterfeit, not as graded, or under a channel's buyer-protection rules):

1. if the card comes back to us undamaged, it returns to your inventory and may be relisted, and the sale is reversed with no Commission;
2. if the refund results from our error, such as our grading, our listing, or the wrong card shipped, we bear the refund costs and any non-refundable fees; and
3. if the refund results from the card itself not matching your representations under Section 2.3, the non-refundable fees and return costs of that sale are deducted from your future proceeds or billed to you.

**6.6 Chargebacks and late claims.** Buyers may keep rights after the inspection period ends, such as card-network chargebacks and marketplace guarantees. The inspection period does not take those rights away. If, after you have been paid, a buyer obtains a chargeback or refund for a reason described in Section 6.5(3), we may deduct the amount paid to you for that card from your future proceeds, or invoice you if no proceeds remain. We will send you the documentation first. We absorb chargebacks caused by fraud we should reasonably have detected, or by our own error.

**6.7 Disputes with us.** If you disagree with a statement, tell us within 60 days of receiving it. We will review the transaction records with you and correct any error.
